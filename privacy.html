Premium Countdown

Privacy policy

Premium Countdown, by TashTashApps. Effective October 26, 2026.

This policy explains what the Premium Countdown app for Android does with your information. In this policy, “we” and “us” means TashTashApps, the maker of the app.

In short

Your countdowns, notes, letters and photos stay on your phone, and in your own Google account’s backup if your phone’s backup is on. The app sends none of them to us. We only hear from you if you choose to write to us.

What the app keeps on your phone

The app keeps what you put into it: your countdowns (titles, dates, times, repeats and notes), the letters you write, the photos you add, and your settings, such as your reminder times and theme.

The app has no internet access of its own. It sends none of this to us or to anyone else. We have no server and no way to see it. The only copy outside your phone is your own backup, explained below.

The app does not use your location. It uses your phone’s time zone to show the right day and time.

Photos

You add photos through Android’s photo picker. The app only sees the photos you choose, so it never asks for permission to read your photo library. It keeps its own copy of each photo you choose, inside the app.

Purchases

This test version of the app sells nothing. It has no payment screen, and it doesn’t connect to Google Play’s payment system. So Google Play tells the app nothing about payments, and we get no sales records about you.

A later version of the app may sell plans through Google Play. If it does, we will update this policy before that version reaches you.

Android backup

When your phone’s backup is turned on, Android copies the app’s data (your countdowns, letters, photos and settings) to your own Google account. Google keeps that copy under its own policy. We can’t see it, and we can’t delete it.

That copy stays in your Google account after you uninstall the app. It stays until you delete it in your Google account’s backup settings. If you install the app again with the same Google account, Android can bring the backup copy back. When you move to a new phone, Android can also copy the app’s data straight across.

Export files

“Export to a file” makes one file with your countdowns, letters and photos, and saves it where you choose. The file stays there until you delete it. Anyone who has the file can open it, so keep it somewhere private.

Sharing and saving

When you share a print or a countdown, your phone’s share menu hands it to the app you pick. What happens next is up to that app. We don’t receive a copy. A print you save to your gallery is an ordinary picture there.

Notifications and alarms

The app uses notifications only for these things: your reminders, a quiet note on the morning of a milestone if you turn on milestone alerts, a note on the big day itself, and a live countdown in a countdown’s final hour if you turn it on for that countdown.

The names of your countdowns can show on your lock screen. To hide them, open Settings in the app and turn on Hide names on the lock screen.

The app uses alarms that ring at the exact minute. You allow them in your phone’s settings. They make reminders arrive on time and keep widgets right in the final day. When your phone restarts, when the app updates, or when the clock, time zone or language changes, the app sets your reminders and widgets right again. It does nothing else at those times.

If you write to us

“Write to a person” in the app opens your own email app with our address filled in. The draft already shows the app version, your Android version and your phone’s make and model, so we can help. You can delete them before you send. Nothing is sent until you press send.

If you write to us, by that button or by email, we receive your email address and what you write. Our mailbox is run by Gmail by Google, which stores the messages for us.

We use your email only to answer you. We never share or sell it. We delete the conversation 12 months after the last message, or sooner if you ask.

What we don’t do

The app has no tools that watch what you do or show you ads. The app does not use an advertising ID. We don’t sell or share your information with anyone.

How your data is kept

Android keeps the app’s files in the app’s own space on your phone, which other apps can’t open. A sealed letter is kept the same way as everything else. “Sealed” means the app won’t show it before the day. It is not locked with a code (encrypted), and changing the phone’s clock can open it early.

Deleting your data

To delete everything the app keeps on your phone, uninstall the app. Or open your phone’s Settings, then Apps, then Premium Countdown, then Storage (or Storage & cache), and tap Clear storage. This also deletes sealed letters, and we can’t bring them back.

A countdown you delete in the app goes to Recently deleted. There you can restore it, or tap Delete forever. After 30 days it is deleted by itself.

Some things are not deleted with the app, and you delete them yourself: the backup copy in your Google account (see Android backup above), any export files, and any prints you saved to your gallery. If you install the app again, Android may bring the backup copy back, so delete that copy first if you want everything gone.

To have us delete emails you sent us, write to tashtashapps@gmail.com.

Your rights

We hold only one kind of information about you: emails you sent us. We keep them so we can answer you.

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to tashtashapps@gmail.com.

If you think we handled your information wrongly, please tell us first. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live. In Israel, that is the Privacy Protection Authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly receive information from children. If a child under 13 has written to us, ask us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version at https://hadadiboys.github.io/premium-countdown/privacy.html with a new effective date, and describe the change in the app’s release notes on Google Play.

Contact
